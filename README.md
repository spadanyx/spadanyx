## About Me

I design and build AI-powered automation systems — workflow engines, integrations, and full-stack applications that turn complex, manual business processes into reliable, scalable software. My work sits at the intersection of AI, automation, software development, and business operations.

I build primarily with **n8n**, **Make**, and **Zapier**, connecting REST APIs, webhooks, CRMs, and LLMs into workflows that hold up in production — not just demos. This includes an AI ticket-classification workflow that returns structured JSON for downstream automation, a full-stack enquiry tracking dashboard on Supabase, and multi-step outreach systems with built-in error handling and retry logic.

My focus is always on designing around the real operational bottleneck and the data quality it depends on — not just stitching tools together. I map the existing process first, decide what's worth automating versus what still needs human judgment, and document the logic so systems stay maintainable after handover.

💼 3+ years of freelance experience, with a hands-on background in business operations that shapes how I scope and design systems

## Featured Projects

**🤖 AI-Powered Ticket Classification Workflow**

n8n workflow that turns unstructured support ticket text into machine-readable classifications — pulls each ticket from Google Sheets, sends it to an LLM with a purpose-built prompt constrained to a defined JSON schema, and feeds the parsed result back into the workflow for automated routing.

`n8n` `Google Gemini` `Google Sheets` `JSON` `Prompt Engineering`

**📋 Full-Stack Enquiry Tracking Dashboard**

Internal tool that replaced enquiries scattered across spreadsheets and inboxes with a single source of truth — custom database schema in Supabase, connected front-end to back-end, and a dashboard for creating, viewing, and updating records end to end.

`Supabase` `PostgreSQL` `CRUD` `Deployment`

**🔄 Business Workflow Automation Systems**

Multi-step automations linking AI models, APIs, spreadsheets, and CRM records into single operational flows — conditional routing, structured data handoffs between steps, and built-in error handling and retry logic so failures surface instead of failing silently.

`n8n` `Make` `Zapier` `Airtable` `Webhooks` `REST APIs`

**📧 Outreach & Campaign Automation System**

Airtable-backed outreach platform built around a single source of truth for contacts, sends, and replies — automated sending, response tracking written back to each record, and weekly reporting with full dashboard visibility.

`Airtable` `Automation Workflows` `Reporting`
